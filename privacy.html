Privacy policy

Studio · Effective 22 November 2026

Studio is a personal tool used only by its owner to manage his own YouTube channel. This page explains what it accesses and what it does with it.

What Studio accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, he can allow Studio to:

  • upload videos to his YouTube channel;
  • view his YouTube account, channel and video details;
  • view YouTube Analytics reports for his channel.

Studio also reads public YouTube data (for example, popular Shorts in India) to suggest ideas.

How it is used

  • Videos are uploaded only after the owner approves each one.
  • Channel statistics are used only to prepare performance reports for the owner.
  • The AI assistant that runs Studio (Anthropic's Claude) may process these statistics and the video text to prepare those reports and scripts for the owner.

Storage and sharing

  • The Google sign-in token and the statistics are stored on the owner's private server, in files only the owner's account can read.
  • Data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with anyone else.

Removing access

The owner can revoke Studio's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored tokens and statistics are deleted from the server on request.

YouTube and Google terms

Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
